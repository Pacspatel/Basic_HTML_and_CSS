1. Strawberry Cream Crepes

Ingredients:

1 cup flour
1 cup milk
2 eggs
1 tablespoon sugar
1 tablespoon melted butter
Strawberries
Whipped cream
Powdered sugar

Steps:

Mix flour, milk, eggs, sugar, and melted butter.
Heat a pan and add a small amount of butter.
Pour a thin layer of batter into the pan.
Cook for about 1–2 minutes on each side.
Add whipped cream and strawberries.
Fold the crepe and sprinkle powdered sugar on top.

Cooking time: About 20 minutes.

2. Chana Masala

Ingredients:

2 cups cooked chickpeas
2 tomatoes, chopped
1 tablespoon oil
1 teaspoon cumin
1 teaspoon turmeric
1 teaspoon chili powder
1 teaspoon garam masala
Salt
Fresh coriander

Steps:

Heat oil in a pan.
Add cumin and cook for a few seconds.
Add chopped tomatoes and cook until soft.
Add turmeric, chili powder, garam masala, and salt.
Add the cooked chickpeas.
Mix well and cook for 8–10 minutes.
Add fresh coriander on top.

Cooking time: About 20 minutes.

3. Maggi

Ingredients:

1 packet Maggi noodles
1½ cups water
Maggi seasoning
Optional: vegetables

Steps:

Boil the water in a pan.
Add the Maggi noodles.
Add the seasoning.
Cook for 2–3 minutes.
Mix well and serve hot.
4. Pasta

Ingredients:

1 cup pasta
2 cups water
½ cup pasta sauce
Salt
Optional: vegetables

Steps:

Boil water and add a little salt.
Add pasta and cook until soft.
Drain the water.
Add pasta sauce and mix well.
Cook for 2 more minutes and serve.


5. Cake

Ingredients:

1 cup flour
½ cup sugar
½ cup milk
¼ cup oil
1 teaspoon baking powder
1 teaspoon vanilla

Steps:

Mix flour, sugar, and baking powder.
Add milk, oil, and vanilla.
Mix until smooth.
Pour into a cake pan.
Bake at 350°F (175°C) for about 25–30 minutes.
Let it cool before serving.


6. Broccoli Subji

Ingredients:

2 cups broccoli
1 tablespoon oil
½ teaspoon cumin
Salt
½ teaspoon turmeric
½ teaspoon chili powder

Steps:

Heat oil in a pan.
Add cumin.
Add broccoli and spices.
Mix everything well.
Cover and cook for 8–10 minutes.
Serve hot with roti or rice.



7. Biscuit Cake

Ingredients:

2 cups biscuits
½ cup milk
2 tablespoons sugar
1 teaspoon baking powder
1 tablespoon cocoa powder

Steps:

Crush the biscuits into small pieces.
Add milk, sugar, cocoa powder, and baking powder.
Mix everything into a smooth batter.
Pour into a cake pan.
Bake at 350°F (175°C) for about 20–25 minutes.
Let it cool and enjoy.


8. Paneer Subji

Ingredients:

1 cup paneer
1 tomato
1 tablespoon oil
½ teaspoon cumin
½ teaspoon turmeric
½ teaspoon chili powder
Salt
½ teaspoon garam masala
½ cup water

Steps:

Cut the paneer into small pieces.
Heat oil in a pan and add cumin.
Add chopped tomato and cook for 2–3 minutes.
Add turmeric, chili powder, and salt.
Add paneer and mix well.
Add water and cook for 5 minutes.
Add garam masala and serve hot with roti or rice.

Cupcake Recipe 🧁

Ingredients:

1 cup flour
½ cup sugar
½ cup milk
¼ cup oil
1 teaspoon baking powder
1 teaspoon vanilla

Steps:

Mix flour, sugar, and baking powder.
Add milk, oil, and vanilla.
Mix well to make a smooth batter.
Pour the batter into cupcake cups.
Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 15–20 minutes.
Let the cupcakes cool and enjoy!


10 Fruit Ice Cream in a Glass 🍓🍨

Ingredients:

1 cup ice cream
½ cup mixed fruits
1 banana
2 tablespoons chocolate syrup
1 tablespoon chopped nuts
Whipped cream (optional)

Steps:

Cut the fruits into small pieces.
Add some fruit to a glass.
Add a scoop of ice cream.
Add another layer of fruit and ice cream.
Add chocolate syrup and chopped nuts.
Top with whipped cream if you like.
Serve immediately and enjoy!
